# ApexCloud Technologies - Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Document Version: 2026.2 (Effective Date: January 1, 2026)

## 1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
ApexCloud offers a full, no-questions-asked 30-day money-back guarantee for all first-time purchases of Annual Pro and Annual Business plans. If you are not completely satisfied within the first 30 days of initial purchase, contact support@apexcloud.io or trigger a human support ticket to receive a 100% refund.

## 2. Monthly Subscription Cancellation
Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time through the Billing Settings page. 
- When you cancel a monthly subscription, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- You will retain full access to your account and data until the current cycle expires.
- We do NOT issue prorated refunds for partial months or unused days within an active monthly subscription cycle.

## 3. Annual Subscription Renewals & Cancellation
Annual subscriptions renew automatically on their anniversary date.
- ApexCloud sends automated email reminders 30 days and 7 days prior to renewal.
- Customers may cancel auto-renewal at any time before the billing renewal date without penalty.
- Refunds for annual renewals requested within 7 calendar days of renewal will be granted with a 10% administrative processing fee. After 7 calendar days, no refunds will be issued for annual renewals.

## 4. Enterprise Contract Terms
Enterprise contracts are governed by their specific Master Services Agreement (MSA). 
- Enterprise agreements require a written cancellation notice at least 60 calendar days prior to contract renewal.
- Early termination fees may apply as stipulated in Section 8.4 of the Enterprise MSA.

## 5. Account Downgrades & Storage Quotas
If you downgrade from a higher tier to a lower tier:
- The downgrade takes effect at the next renewal cycle.
- If your account exceeds the storage quota of the lower tier (e.g. 500 GB on Pro vs 10 GB on Starter), you will have a 14-day grace period to download or remove excess data. After 14 days, files exceeding the quota will be read-only.

## 6. Chargebacks and Disputes
We strongly urge customers to contact our support team before initiating a chargeback with their bank or credit card provider. Accounts with open dispute claims will be temporarily frozen pending investigation.
